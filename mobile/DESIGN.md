\## Brand \& Style



The design system embodies a \*\*High-Energy Modern\*\* aesthetic, specifically tailored for a youth-oriented, live-streaming radio environment. The brand personality is electric, immediate, and authoritative, evoking the "always-on" nature of broadcast media.



We utilize a \*\*Modern Corporate\*\* foundation blended with \*\*High-Contrast\*\* accents to ensure maximum visibility and engagement. The UI prioritizes content immersion, using deep dark-mode surfaces to make vibrant broadcast indicators and playback controls pop. The emotional response is one of excitement and professional reliability—a digital translation of a high-end radio studio.



\## Colors



The palette is derived directly from the vibrancy of live broadcasting. 



\*   \*\*Gaul Green (Primary):\*\* A deep, saturated emerald used for primary brand moments, success states, and key navigational highlights.

\*   \*\*On-Air Orange (Secondary):\*\* An energetic, warm orange reserved for interactive calls-to-action, streaming controls, and progress indicators.

\*   \*\*Accent Red (Tertiary):\*\* A pure, high-visibility red used exclusively for 'LIVE' indicators and urgent system alerts.

\*   \*\*Surface Hierarchy:\*\* Surfaces are built on a spectrum of deep charcoal and true black (`#050505` to `#1A1C1E`) to provide a sophisticated, studio-like backdrop that eliminates visual noise.



\## Typography



This design system uses \*\*Plus Jakarta Sans\*\* exclusively to maintain a cohesive, modern, and high-energy feel. The type scale is optimized for legibility against dark backgrounds. 



Headlines use heavy weights (Bold/ExtraBold) with tight tracking to mimic the impactful typography of broadcast news and event posters. Body text maintains a comfortable weight (Regular) to ensure long-form readability during show descriptions or news feeds. Labels, particularly for "LIVE" status or "ON AIR," utilize uppercase styling and bold weights to command immediate attention.



\## Layout \& Spacing



The layout follows a \*\*Fluid Grid\*\* model with high-density spacing to reflect the fast-paced nature of radio.



\-   \*\*Desktop:\*\* 12-column grid with 24px gutters. Content is centered with wide 64px margins to allow the UI to breathe.

\-   \*\*Mobile:\*\* 4-column grid with 16px margins. 

\-   \*\*Rhythm:\*\* An 8px linear scale governs all padding and margins. Vertical rhythm is tight for control clusters (e.g., playback bars) and expansive for content sections (e.g., hero carousels) to create clear visual hierarchy.



\## Elevation \& Depth



Hierarchy is established through \*\*Tonal Layers\*\* rather than heavy shadows. In a dark-mode environment, depth is conveyed by shifting surface brightness:



1\.  \*\*Background (Level 0):\*\* Pure black `#050505`.

2\.  \*\*Surface (Level 1):\*\* Deep charcoal `#121417` for main card containers.

3\.  \*\*Raised (Level 2):\*\* Lighter grey `#1E2023` for hovered states or active controls.



Where overlays are necessary (e.g., mobile menus or playback modifiers), a \*\*Backdrop Blur\*\* (Glassmorphism) of 20px with a 10% white tint is used to maintain context of the background broadcast content while ensuring foreground readability.



\## Shapes



The design system utilizes \*\*Rounded\*\* geometry to balance the aggressive typography and high-contrast colors. 



\-   \*\*Standard Elements:\*\* Buttons and input fields use a 0.5rem (8px) radius.

\-   \*\*Containers:\*\* Content cards and player modules use `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-xl` (24px) to create a distinct, friendly silhouette against the dark background.

\-   \*\*Interactive Indicators:\*\* Small status chips (like the 'LIVE' tag) use pill-shaped (full-round) corners to differentiate them from functional buttons.



\## Components



\### Buttons

\-   \*\*Primary:\*\* Gaul Green background with white text. High-gloss finish optional for hero actions.

\-   \*\*Secondary/Streaming:\*\* On-Air Orange background. Used specifically for "Play," "Listen Now," or "Join Stream."

\-   \*\*Ghost:\*\* Transparent background with white or green borders for secondary navigation.



\### Live Indicator

\-   A pill-shaped chip using \*\*Accent Red\*\*. It must include a pulsing animation to indicate active status.



\### Input Fields

\-   Dark-filled backgrounds (`#1A1C1E`) with a 1px border that glows \*\*On-Air Orange\*\* when focused.



\### Cards

\-   Cards utilize a subtle gradient from top-left to bottom-right (Deep Grey to Black) to suggest volume. Borders are minimal or non-existent, relying on tonal separation.



\### Playback Bar

\-   Always anchored to the bottom. Uses a semi-transparent glass effect to allow content to scroll behind it. The progress bar is rendered in \*\*On-Air Orange\*\*.
